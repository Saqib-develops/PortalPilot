Scholarship Eligibility & Guidelines

Post-matric and pre-matric scholarships are open for eligible students belonging to SC, ST, OBC, General, and Minority categories in Uttar Pradesh.

  • Annual family income must not exceed INR 2,50,000 for SC/ST and INR 2,00,000 for General/OBC.
  • Applicant must be enrolled in a recognized UP educational institution.
